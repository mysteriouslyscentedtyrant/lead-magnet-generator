FAQ

Common questions.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no signup and no payment. To keep it free for everyone, each visitor can create a few lead magnets per hour.

What can I create?

An ebook (introduction, 4 to 7 chapters and a conclusion), a step-by-step guide (the problem, the method, common mistakes and the next step), or a one-page checklist. Each one ends with a call to action you can link to your offer.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Yes. You review and edit the outline before anything is written: headings, key points and the order of chapters. After writing, you can rewrite any section, and change the title, subtitle and call to action whenever you like.

To edit individual sentences, use "Copy text" and paste the content into your own editor.

How do I get the PDF?

Click "Download PDF" and your file downloads in a few seconds, with a call-to-action button readers can click.

If the download service is busy, you can still save it with your browser's print window: choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Is my work saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device, so you can close the page and come back. It stays until you start a new lead magnet or clear your browser data.

What happens to the information I type?

Your topic, audience, offer and outline are sent to an AI model to write the content. They are not kept after the content is written.

When you download, your finished document (including your logo) is sent to our PDF service to create the file, which deletes it automatically afterwards. Until then, everything stays in your browser.

Can I trust the content?

The AI is told not to invent statistics, studies or quotes, and to mark examples as hypothetical. AI can still make mistakes, so read your lead magnet before you share it.
